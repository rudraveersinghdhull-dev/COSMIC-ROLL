How to Play

🎲 Rolling

Press ROLL any time — it's free. There are over 10,000 Auras across 9 tiers, from Common up through Secret, plus one absolute one-in-a-lifetime tier above all of them (see MONARCH below). Your Luck level and any active Biome shift the odds toward rarer tiers.

🌍 Biomes

A rotating Biome is always active — shown at the top of the Roll screen — and changes every 10 minutes. Each Biome temporarily boosts one specific tier's odds. Time it right and lean into the boosted tier.

🍀 Luck & 🧬 Mutation Chance

Spend Coins to raise your Luck level (shifts odds toward rarer tiers) and your Mutation Chance (odds that a roll gets a random rarity-multiplying Mutation, like "LUCKY 10x"). Both are separate upgrades on the Roll screen.

👑 MONARCH

One single Aura in the entire game sits in its own Transcended tier at odds of 1 / 3,000,000,000. If you ever roll it, you'll see a number count up live before MONARCH is revealed. Good luck.

🎯 Equip

Equip any Aura you've discovered from the Index, or right after rolling it, to show it off as your main Aura and use it by default in Flex Battle.

📖 Index

The Index shows every Aura and its name, tier, and odds — even ones you haven't rolled yet, so you know what to chase. Search by name.

⚔️ Flex Battle

Unlocks after discovering 3 Auras. Pick an Aura and face an NPC — rarer Aura wins the point (Mutations count too). Switch your Aura between rounds. First to 3 points wins Coins. NPCs get tougher — and occasionally mutated themselves — as your NPC level climbs.

🧵 Crafting & 🎒 Inventory

Rolling earns tier "Shards." Spend Shards + Coins in the Craft tab on 56 recipes — 31 unique Potions (coins, temporary Luck/Mutation boosts, guaranteed tiers, Shard refunds, instant NPC level skips, or the ultimate Heavenly Potion) and 25 Gauntlets (equip one for a permanent battle-reward bonus AND a Luck bonus). Your inventory holds 20 item slots by default, upgradeable up to 200.

➕ Create Custom Aura

Design your own Aura from Settings — name, tier, odds, and colour — and it's instantly added to the Index and rollable.

📅 Daily Quests & 🏆 Achievements

Three quests reset every day for bonus Coins. Achievements (10,000+) auto-unlock as you discover Auras and hit milestones — check Settings for both.

⚡ Quick Roll

An instant, no-animation roll mode — unlockable in Settings for 10,000,000 Coins.
